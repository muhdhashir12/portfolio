THE DRIVER PROFILE

MORE THAN JUST
THE DRIVE.

I am Hashir, a dedicated driver and automotive enthusiast whose life revolves around the pulse of high-performance engines, precision control, and raw mechanical feedback.

My driving identity spans two distinct realms of automotive passion: the calculated precision and tire-smoking aggression of drifting, alongside the raw, unfiltered adrenaline of fast driving on open stretches. When tarmac ends, the adventure continues in the grit of mud racing.

Away from the steering wheel, I find deep inspiration in cinematic storytelling and movies—particularly automotive choreography and atmospheric film scores that mirror the intensity of the cockpit.
